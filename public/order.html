      ✳ How it works

      From click to counter.

      Three quiet steps. The register does the loud part.

      01

      Order online

      Customers pick seeds and merch, choose a pickup window, and place the order. No account, no card, no fuss.

      02

      Lands in the POS queue

      Each order gets a number and a ticket, then waits in the sync queue with a clear status. Nothing gets lost.

      03

      Syncs to the register

      One click pushes queued orders to Square, Clover, Toast, Shopify POS or your own webhook. Pay at pickup, totals already match.
